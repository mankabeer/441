441 is not a networking event. Not a dinner party. Not a curated experience or a conversation series.

441 is four people who agree, for one evening, to be present — with each other and with themselves. The format is the philosophy. Four, because five becomes a crowd. One purpose, because intention gives meaning to presence.

It was designed for people who usually leave early. For the ones who stand near walls, who prefer depth to breadth, who find most social settings quietly exhausting.

441 is not for everyone. It was never meant to be.

4

Never more. Never less. Four is the smallest number that creates a room. Below four, it's a conversation. Above four, it becomes a crowd.
