About

Four.
Four.
One.

441 is not a dinner party. Not networking with candles. Not a curated experience or a structured programme.

441 is four people who agree, for one evening, to be fully present — with each other and with themselves.

The format is the philosophy. Four, because five becomes an audience. One purpose, because intention gives meaning to presence.

It was built for people who usually leave early. For the ones who stand near walls, who prefer depth to breadth, who find most gatherings quietly exhausting.

441 is not for everyone. It was never meant to be.

The Structure
4
People

Never more. Never less. Four is the smallest number that creates a room. Below four, it becomes a conversation. Above four, a crowd.

4
Corners

Each person holds a corner of the table. Equal presence, equal weight. No seat of honour. No head of the table. A geometry of peers.

Purpose
